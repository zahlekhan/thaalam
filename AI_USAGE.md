## Product use of AI

Thaalam calls a vision model at runtime to read each photo. This is a product feature, separate from AI-assisted development: Jev (TypeSafe hosted API) by default, and vegaml (local open model) for the laptop demo. The models only answer fixed choice questions (`QUESTIONS` in `server/app.py`); the music is generated by code.
