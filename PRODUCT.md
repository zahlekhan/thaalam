# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

Runs in the browser on phones and laptops. There is no native app.

## Users

**Primary: a small group of friends jamming together.** They are in the same physical room, each on their own phone or laptop, adding photos to one shared canvas through a room link. Nobody needs to know music. The group's job is to make something together that sounds like their photos and to play with it: toss photos in, mute some, delete others, try another photo.

**Also served:**

- A person playing solo, often on a phone, making a loop from their own camera roll. The experience is the same as a room with one person in it.
- Judges at Devin Hacks Kochi (Oct 24 to 25, 2026), Track 4: Make Room for Play. They see a short live demo.

The Code view exposes the generated Strudel pattern for anyone curious. Musicians are not a confirmed target audience.

## Product Purpose

Drop photos on a canvas and hear them become one original music loop. Each photo becomes a card that plays one part of the loop. Every card on the canvas plays unless it is muted.

Success means a group goes from opening a shared link to hearing a loop made from their own photos quickly, and keeps playing by rearranging cards. Horizon: the live demo has to land at the hackathon on Oct 25, 2026, and the product continues after the event. Work done for the demo should not be throwaway.

## Positioning

**A photo is a stem.** Each photo always plays the same part (its role), so people mix photos the way producers mix stems: drop a photo in to bring its part in, mute it to drop it out, delete it to remove it. A card joins on the next bar.

**The model reads; code writes the music.** A vision model answers fixed multiple-choice questions about each photo (mood, energy, texture, role). Code turns those answers plus pixel measurements (brightness, warmth, busyness, palette) into a Strudel pattern from small hand-written pattern menus. No audio is generated by a model, the loops are original, and the same photo gives the same part.

## Operating Context

- **Shared room:** a link joins a room. Everyone sees the same canvas and cards and can add, move or remove any card. Each device plays the loop on its own clock; timing is not synced across devices.
- **Starting sound:** browsers need a tap before audio plays ("Tap to start sound").
- **Adding photos:** drag and drop, paste, the file picker ("Add photo"), or a tray of 6 sample photos with precomputed readings so they play instantly.
- **Devices:** phones (including rotation) and laptops; the canvas refits to the window.
- **The canvas is a deliberate mess:** cards land and stay wherever people put them, scattered and overlapping like photos thrown on a table. There is no stage frame, grid or lanes to organize them into.
- **Card actions:** drag a card to move it anywhere. Click or tap a card to get its actions: mute or unmute, and delete.
- **First run:** a one-time notice explains that photos are shared with the room and read by an AI model, and that nudity and profanity are not allowed.
- **Developer mode:** `?dev` in the URL shows a picker for the reading model.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Terminology** (use these words in UI and docs):

- **Card:** a photo plus its reading and the part it plays.
- **Reading:** what we learn about a photo. Code measures pixels; the model reads mood, energy, texture and role.
- **Role:** kick, percussion, bass, chords, melody or texture. Fixed per photo.
- **Canvas:** the shared surface where cards are scattered. Position has no musical meaning.
- **Muted:** a card that stays on the canvas but is silent. Unmute it to bring its part back.
- **Mix:** every unmuted card, sharing one tempo, key and scale.
- **Room:** a shared canvas that several people join through a link.

**Behavior that future work must keep:**

- Input is images only. No video and no audio export.
- Mix tempo comes from average energy (80 to 128 BPM). Scale comes from a mood vote. Two cards with the same role take turns every 2 bars.
- Moderation: each photo is checked by the model before it is shared. A flagged photo is removed and the uploader sees a one-line reason. Until a photo clears, other people in the room see a "Listening..." card, never the image.
- If the model can't be reached, the card plays from pixel measurements alone and is labeled as a guess. Failures are shown, never hidden behind fake readings.
- Loops are original. Never suggest or imitate existing songs.
- No backward compatibility: adding instruments may change how old photos sound.

**Technical constraints:**

- React 19, TypeScript and Vite in `web/`. The canvas is React Flow (`@xyflow/react`), which replaced tldraw in commit `9fb7a3c`. Sound is Strudel (`@strudel/web`, AGPL-3.0), so the project stays open source.
- Rooms sync with Yjs on a Cloudflare Durable Object (`worker/index.js`). The hosted app is one Cloudflare Worker on a single origin.
- Readings come from two providers behind one question set: Jev (TypeSafe hosted API, the default) and vegaml (a local open model, available only on a laptop, not on Cloudflare).

**Decided, not yet fully built** (decided by the user on 2026-10-24):

- **Replacing the stage.** The user chose to remove the stage frame and parking. The current code still uses them, and the redesign will replace them with the scattered canvas and the mute and delete actions described above.
- **Canvases are kept permanently.** Rooms and their photos don't expire (photos are stored in R2). A canvas appears in the public gallery at `/gallery` only when someone in the room chooses to publish it.
- **Names in rooms.** People joining a room can enter a name, or get a random one if they don't. Names are shown to others in the room (for example when someone joins). There are no accounts.

**Open decisions** (not settled; don't present them as settled):

- Key choice (fixed for now), per-card volume (there is no control for it yet), and cross-device timing sync.
- No accessibility standard has been set.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** Thaalam (Malayalam for rhythm). **The name is the only commitment.** The product is for anyone, anywhere, and future copy, content and sample curation should not emphasize a Kerala or Malayalam identity. Some current sample photos show Kerala scenes; they are sample content, not a brand signal.
- **Disclosure:** libraries, APIs and photo credits are disclosed (README, `web/public/samples/CREDITS.md`). Keep doing this as dependencies change.
- **Existing copy** is short, plain and friendly ("Tap to start sound", "Try one", "Drop a photo on the stage", "Listening..."). That is what the UI says today, not a voice the user has confirmed.

## Product Principles

1. **Together first.** Design for several people on several devices around one canvas. Solo play is the same experience with one person, not a separate mode.
2. **No musical skill required.** Arranging photos is the whole instrument. Music theory stays in code; the Code view is optional curiosity, never a requirement.
3. **Stable cause and effect.** The same photo always plays the same part; unmuted it plays and muted it's silent. Make the consequences of each move easy to hear and see.
4. **Show what's really happening.** Make listening, guessed, rejected and muted states visible. Never fake a reading or hide a failure.
5. **Demo now, built to last.** The first minute has to land live for judges, without shortcuts that won't survive as a real product.
